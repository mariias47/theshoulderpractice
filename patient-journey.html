What happens when you come to us

From your first call to your full recovery — here is what to expect at every stage.

1

Getting in touch

You can self-refer — no GP letter is required. Call or email our practice team, who will take brief details about your problem and find the most suitable consultant for your case. We will confirm your appointment, your location, any imaging you should bring, and your insurer details if applicable. Most patients are seen within 1 or 2 working days.

2

Your first consultation

Your first appointment is typically 30 minutes. Your consultant will take a thorough history, examine you carefully, and review any scans or letters you have brought. This appointment is never rushed. You will leave with a clear understanding of what we think is wrong, what your options are, and what we recommend — with full explanation of the reasoning behind it.

3

Imaging & investigation

If we need further information — an MRI, ultrasound, or X-ray — we will arrange this directly, often at the same site. We work with specialist musculoskeletal radiologists whose reporting we trust. Results are reviewed promptly and communicated clearly.

4

Treatment decision

Some patients leave their first appointment with a treatment plan that doesn't involve surgery — physiotherapy, injections, or monitored rest. Others will be advised that surgery is the right course. In either case, the decision is yours, made with full information and without pressure. We will give you our honest recommendation; you decide what happens next.

5

Surgery (where applicable)

Surgery is performed at one of our partner hospitals — all of them leading private facilities in London. You will be told exactly what the procedure involves, what anaesthesia will be used, how long it takes, and what the first days of recovery look like. Your consultant performs your operation personally.

6

Recovery & rehabilitation

We work with expert physiotherapists — either those embedded within our locations or those you already work with — to design a structured rehabilitation programme. Your progress is monitored at follow-up appointments, and we are accessible between appointments if concerns arise.

If at any stage you are unsure, want a second opinion, or simply want to talk through your options before committing to anything — that is what we are here for.
