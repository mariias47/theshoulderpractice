Research, teaching and the advancement of upper limb surgery

The Shoulder Practice is not only a place where treatment happens — it is a place where the specialty moves forward. Our surgeons hold or have held academic appointments, contribute to peer-reviewed research, and teach at regional, national and international level.

As a group, we organise The London Shoulder Meeting, an annual international scientific meeting that hosts top professionals in the field to discuss recent advances in the specialty.

That engagement with the wider field directly benefits patients: it means the techniques used here are current, the evidence base is scrutinised, and the standards applied are those we ourselves helped to shape.

Research areas

  • Outcomes in rotator cuff repair — measuring long-term function rather than short-term symptom resolution
  • Shoulder instability in the young athletic population — surgical timing and recurrence
  • Revision shoulder arthroplasty — case series and implant selection
  • Further areas to be confirmed
