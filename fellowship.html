A practice built to train the best

The Shoulder Practice has always been more than a clinic.

From the beginning, Mr Cobiella built his practice with the deliberate intention of bringing exceptional young surgeons into its orbit — giving them the case volume, the mentorship and the clinical environment they need to become genuinely outstanding upper limb specialists.

This isn't a training programme in the conventional sense. There are no cohorts, no fixed curricula. What we offer is something rarer: sustained, direct exposure to one of the most experienced upper limb surgeons in the country, across the full breadth of elective and complex reconstructive work.

Fellows who have trained here have gone on to consultant posts at leading NHS and private institutions across the UK. Some have remained within The Shoulder Practice, deepening a collaboration that began as mentorship.

What you gain

Exposure to high-volume, complex upper limb surgery across shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand — including revision cases and failed-surgery reconstructions that few training environments can offer.

How it works

Fellows work directly alongside Mr Cobiella and the wider team in clinic and theatre. The relationship is close, demanding and genuinely formative. Duration is typically six months to a year, tailored to the individual.

Who we're looking for

Surgeons at senior registrar or post-CCT level with a serious commitment to upper limb as a specialty. Academic interest, research appetite and a collaborative instinct matter as much as technical ability.

If you are considering upper limb as your subspecialty and want to train in an environment where standards are uncompromising and the work is genuinely interesting, we would like to hear from you.
