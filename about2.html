Thirty years in the making

The Shoulder Practice did not begin in a boardroom. It began with a conviction - that the fragmented, impersonal nature of specialist orthopaedic care in the UK could be done better. That patients deserved not just technical competence, but continuity, honesty and time.

Mr Carlos Cobiella spent the better part of three decades building that conviction into something real. Operating at the highest level of NHS and private practice, training some of the country's finest upper limb surgeons, and quietly assembling a network of colleagues who shared his standards.

When The Shoulder Practice was formally established, it was not a startup. It was the natural culmination of a career built on doing things properly.

What makes us different

A philosophy, not a platform

Private upper limb care in London is not in short supply. What is rarer is a practice where the clinical culture has been shaped by a single, sustained philosophy - one built over decades of complex operative work and difficult referrals.

What guides us

Clinical excellence

Evidence-based, technically uncompromising care - delivered by surgeons at the top of their field.

Unhurried judgement

We give every patient the time they deserve. Good decisions require information, and information requires time.

Honest guidance

We tell patients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. Clarity is a form of care.

Continuity of care

The surgeon who diagnoses you is the surgeon who treats you. Consistency is not a luxury - it is a clinical necessity.
