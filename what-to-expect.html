What happens when you come to us

From first call to full recovery - here is what to expect

Getting in touch

Self-refer directly, no GP letter needed. We'll take brief details, find the right consultant, and confirm everything you need. Most patients are seen within two working days.

Your first consultation

Your first appointment is 30 minutes and never rushed. Your consultant will examine you, review your scans, and give you a clear answer, with full explanation of the reasoning.

Imaging & investigation

If we need further information - an MRI, ultrasound, or X-ray - we will arrange this directly, often at the same site. We work with specialist musculoskeletal radiologists whose reporting we trust. Results are reviewed promptly and communicated clearly.

Treatment decision

Some patients leave with a plan that doesn't involve surgery. Others are advised it's the right course. Either way, the decision is yours - made with full information, without pressure.

Surgery (where applicable)

Surgery takes place at leading private facilities in London. You'll be told exactly what to expect - procedure, anaesthesia, recovery. Your consultant operates personally.

Recovery & rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation, designed around you - with expert physiotherapists either within our locations or your own. Progress is monitored and we remain accessible throughout.

We're here whenever you're ready, even if that's just to talk
