Consultations & diagnosis

Your first appointment is a thorough, unhurried assessment – for patients of all ages, including children. We'll examine you, review any previous scans, and arrange specialist MRI or ultrasound imaging if needed. You'll leave with a clear diagnosis and all your options explained.

Non-surgical treatments

Surgery is not always the answer. We offer guided physiotherapy, steroid injections for pain and inflammation, and PRP injections for tendon conditions. Many patients achieve full recovery without ever needing an operation.

Surgery

When surgery is the best option, you'll be in the hands of surgeons who perform these procedures at high volume, in world-class facilities – from keyhole arthroscopic surgery to complex joint reconstruction and revision of previous failed surgery.

Rehabilitation & physiotherapy

Recovery doesn't end when you leave the operating theatre. We work alongside expert physiotherapists to get you back to full strength and confidence, whether you've had surgery or not.

Expertise

We specialise in diagnosing and treating a wide range of shoulder conditions, including arthritis, instability, rotator cuff injuries, labral tears, frozen shoulder, fractures and failed previous surgeries.

Whether you're dealing with pain, impingement, tendon issues or complex surgical cases, we offer expert care to restore function and relieve discomfort.
