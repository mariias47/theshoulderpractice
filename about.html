Exceptional upper limb care, right from the start

Founded in 2017, The Shoulder Practice was built with a single purpose: to offer world-class diagnosis, treatment and research for injuries and conditions of the upper body – from the fingertips to the shoulder joint.

We bring together an expert network of surgeons, radiologists, anaesthetists and physiotherapists, giving every patient access to a truly comprehensive, joined-up service. Whether you're navigating a complex shoulder condition, a sports injury, or considering joint replacement, you are in the most capable hands.
