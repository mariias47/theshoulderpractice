Thirty years in the making

The Shoulder Practice was formally established in 2017. But its foundations go back considerably further.

For over 25 years, Mr Carlos Cobiella built one of the most respected upper limb practices in the United Kingdom — treating complex cases, training surgeons, and developing a reputation that drew patients from across the country and beyond. The Shoulder Practice is the formal expression of everything that practice stood for: a named home for a standard of care that had long existed, now with the structure and team to deliver it at scale.

What makes us different

Private upper limb care in London is not in short supply. What is rarer is a practice where the clinical culture has been shaped by a single, sustained philosophy — one built over 25 years of complex operative work, difficult referrals and the kind of outcomes that generate word-of-mouth reputation rather than marketing spend.

We are not a network. We are not a referral platform. Every surgeon associated with The Shoulder Practice has been personally selected and, in most cases, trained within the practice. The standard you receive from any member of this team reflects the same values — clinical rigour, honest communication, and a commitment to getting the outcome right first time.

The patient at the centre

We see patients who have been told nothing is wrong when something clearly is. Patients who have had surgery elsewhere that hasn't worked. Patients who are professional athletes with a career on the line, or individuals in their sixties who simply want to lift their grandchild without pain. The complexity of the case doesn't change the approach: a thorough assessment, a straight answer, and a treatment plan that is right for that person — not the most convenient or most profitable one.

Our values

Clinical excellence

We operate at the top of our specialty. Our surgeons hold or have held consultant posts at University College London Hospital, leading London private hospitals and major Teaching Hospital NHS trusts. We maintain academic engagement with the latest research, techniques and implants — and we are honest when evidence doesn't yet support a new approach.

Unhurried judgement

The first consultation is never a formality. We take the time to examine properly, review imaging carefully, and explain clearly. Patients leave knowing what is wrong, what their options are, and what we recommend — and why.

Honest guidance

We will tell you when surgery is not the answer. We will tell you when previous treatment has left a problem that can be corrected. We will tell you what realistic recovery looks like. That honesty is not a courtesy — it is the foundation of good clinical care.

Continuity of care

From first consultation through surgery, rehabilitation and follow-up, you are looked after by people who know your case. You will not be passed between strangers. Your surgeon knows your name before you walk in.
