Fees & insurance

Insurance

The Shoulder Practice works with all major private medical insurers, including BUPA, AXA Health, Aviva, Vitality, Cigna, Allianz, WPA. If you are using insurance, please contact your insurer before your appointment to obtain an authorisation number and confirm your cover. Our team is happy to assist if you are unsure of the process.

Self-pay

We welcome self-funding patients. Consultation fees are £400 for an initial appointment and £300 for follow-up. Surgery costs are provided as a fixed quote following your consultation, covering surgeon, anaesthetist and hospital facility fees. There are no hidden costs.

Overseas patients

We regularly treat patients travelling from outside the United Kingdom. Our practice team can coordinate your appointments, imaging and surgery around your visit, and liaise with your clinical team at home regarding aftercare. Please contact us directly to discuss your situation.
