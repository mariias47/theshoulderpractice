When performance is what's at stake

For athletes, performers and professionals whose livelihood depends on physical capability, a shoulder, elbow, wrist or hand injury is never just a medical problem. It is a career problem. A contract problem. A question of whether — and when — you can return to the level that defines you.

We understand that calculus. We have been managing high-performance patients for over 25 years — working within the timelines of competitive seasons, performance schedules and professional obligations, and making treatment decisions that account for the whole picture, not just the scan.

Rapid access

We know that waiting matters. We offer priority appointments, rapid imaging and swift treatment decisions — often within days of first contact.

Discretion as standard

Your attendance at this practice is entirely private. We do not discuss patients, publicise associations or seek profile from the people we treat. The same standards that govern our clinical work govern our confidentiality.

Return-to-performance planning

Surgery, where needed, is planned around your professional timeline wherever this is clinically possible. Rehabilitation is intensive, structured and monitored. We work with your existing performance support team — coaches, physiotherapists, nutritionists — or can recommend from our own trusted network.

Second opinions on previous treatment

We regularly see high-performance patients who have received treatment elsewhere and are not recovering as expected. If something isn't right, we will tell you plainly — and where there is a path to better function, we will find it.

If you are managing an injury under professional pressure, or advising a client, athlete or performer who is, we would welcome a direct conversation.
